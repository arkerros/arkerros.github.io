REDSHIFT Privacy Policy & Copyright

Effective 15 November 2026 · Arkerros

This Privacy Policy explains what information REDSHIFT, a game published by Arkerros, handles and who receives it.

Game data on your device

REDSHIFT is an offline space-flight game. Game progress, settings and earned credits are stored only on your device. The game has no accounts, and Arkerros does not operate a game server: this data is never sent to Arkerros. Deleting the app or clearing its storage removes the local save.

What Arkerros collects

Arkerros does not itself collect, receive or store personal information through the game. The game contains no Arkerros analytics, no sign-in and no in-app purchases, and it does not access your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Advertising

REDSHIFT contains no advertising or advertising SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Arkerros holds no personal information about you, so there is nothing for Arkerros to delete; if you have a request or a question about your data, contact us and we will help.

Security

The app communicates only over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and does not permit unencrypted traffic. Android cloud backup of the app's data is turned off. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

REDSHIFT is not directed to children under 13, or under the higher age of digital consent that applies in your country, and Arkerros does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us.

Contact

Contact Arkerros with privacy or copyright questions at arkerros@gmail.com or through Instagram or X.

We may update this policy as the app and its services change. Review the effective date above.
